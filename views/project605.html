My Garage
‹
›
This is my garage. It is a work in progess and will probably never be completely done. I add things or revise the layout often when something new comes to mind. I built all of the cabinetry and storage shelves to maximize the small amount of space I have; I've accumulated a lot of tools over the years and am always looking for excuses to get more. My tablesaw, which is over 30 years old, is by far my favorite and the most versitle in my shop.

About Me and this Site

Hi, I’m John Chleborad – welcome to my corner of the web.

I build stuff out of wood, I create things with leather, I ride a bike, I play some golf and I have a simple website that I created and work on occasionally. But most important to me is my family. I am a husband to an incredible woman, Cindy, and a father to two of the most amazing people I have ever met, Danyel and Tyler. Without the presence of those three people in my life, I would not be complete. As I write this we are three weeks away from welcoming another into our family, and I cannot be more excited for Tyler and Kirsten.

Cindy and I love exploring the State of Arizona and other parts of the country with each other. We like day trips around Phoenix and Arizona, we like short weekend getaways, and we love long drives across the country. When presented with a choice, we prefer to drive rather than fly—there’s something special about the time we spend together watching the landscape unfold mile by mile. It’s not just about the destination for us; it’s about the journey.

Family is everything. We’ve been blessed with two amazing kids, Danyel and Tyler, and nothing brings us more joy than spending time with them and watching them grow into the wonderful people they are. They are beyond successful in anything they put their minds to. They both have wonderful careers, have established themselves as leaders and surround themselves with people and friends who value relationship over individual.

Building this website has been a journey for me. As with most projects I take on, I over-think and under-do. I started it shortly after I completed my Software Development Certification in 2018, and 7 years later (as this is being written), I am just starting to put together a design that I can feel good about. Life gets in the way, my CDO starts to take over and I get overwhelmed to the point I don’t get started.

I wasn’t sure how the site should look or what to include, but I think now I have something I can continue to build upon. The most important page for me is Cindy & Me. It is a project I told Cindy I would do but never got it off the ground. I even bought a domain in 2018, cindyandme.com but never used it. It only occurred to me a couple of weeks ago that I could just add a page on this site. See my reference to overthinking above.

In my early life I played baseball. I pitched for Rapid City Post 22, represented USA Baseball, earning a Silver Medal in the 1983 World Friendship Series. I was on the starting rotation as a freshman at San Diego State University—GO AZTECS! An injury sidelined me, but my experiences taught me discipline, resilience, and teamwork—values I still carry with me every day.

God blesses me in every way possible every day, and I am a firm believer that there is a Plan for everyone. I may not know how it ends, but I know it will be good. I love my wife, I love my family, and I would not have anything any other way.

Thank you for stopping by and getting to know me a little better. I hope this site gives you a glimpse of who I am and what I care about.

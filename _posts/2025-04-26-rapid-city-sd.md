<p>
    In April We flew from Phoenix to Rapid City to celebrate Mom’s 90th birthday. It was a short trip, (we left on Friday and returned Monday), but it was a fun and memorable time
</p>

<p>
	Friday afternoon we made the obligatory stop for lunch at Taco John's. Not Cindy's favorite (understatement!), but we always make sure to get taco's for John when we visit Rapid.
</p>

<p>
	Saturday all of the Chleborads and Stoners gathered together fun and games and to spend time with Mom. Austin wasn't able to make the trip and we missed having him around, but he was in all of our thoughts all day long.  Along with family, a few
	friends stopped by as well.
<p>
	We played games.  We laughed and we had great conversations, remembering life growing 	up and talking about the future.
</p>
	
<p>
	Brian made gifts for the nephews, Danyel made t-shirts with trivia from 1934 and we played beer (water) pong in the garage for what seemed like hours!
</p>

<p> This trip was much needed for us and we always love visiting Rapid City (just not in 
	the winter!).  It is good to see those that we love so much, and we don't get that opportunity often enough.
</p>

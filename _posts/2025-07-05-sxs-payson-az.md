<p>
    In July of 2024 we took a leap and purchased a 2021 Wildcat Arctic Cat Trail SxS with the intent of taking in out on trails around Arizona and exploring our great state.  We rented one a couple of years prior and really had a great time, so we started looking to buy one so we could go wherever whenever.  We just never found the time, life got in the way and we have no space to store a trailer, so we rely on rentals, but they are hard to come by when you have the time.  
</p>

<p>
	Fast forward to July of 2025, and this is the first time we had a chance to take it out.  All the stars aligned; we had the time, a rental was available, and the weather was perfect!
</p>

<p>
	We decided on the Pyeatt OHV Trail just outside of Payson.  It is about 25 miles, is moderate in difficulty, and gave us an opportunity to go be amongst the trees and streams.  It was a fun ride.  We stopped for a few pictures, dipped our feet in the cool stream, had a small picnic lunch and overall had a really good time.
<p>
	Along the way, we stumbled upon a winery called Below The Rim Meadery.  It was tucked away in a place that felt so remote you wondered how they managed to stay open.  The people were very nice, the food was great and it was a perfect way to spend some time just listening to the wind through the trees.  Highly recommended if you are ever in the area.
</p>
	
<p> Cindy loves the mountains, the smell of the pine trees and listening to the wind rustling through the trees.  I really enjoy sitting by a mountain stream and watching the water cascade over the rocks and obstacles.  This was a great trip for the first adventure out with our side x side.
</p>
